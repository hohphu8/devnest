# DevNest 0.2.18

## Highlights

- Faster startup, navigation, service actions, and scheduled-task history.
- More reliable Apache/Nginx startup with WSL port-conflict recovery.

## Improved

- Runtime discovery now runs in the background and reuses cached results.
- Scheduled-task history is indexed and automatically retains the latest 1,000 runs per task.
- App routes and browser-preview mocks load on demand, while managed web logs rotate automatically.
- Web-server startup validates process ownership and retries transient boot failures.

## Notes

- The first launch cleans old scheduled-task history in small background batches.
- Use **Shutdown WSL & Retry** when WSL owns a managed web-server port.
